## 1. Macroeconomic & Workforce Context: C&SD National Accounts & Four Key Industries

Under Hong Kong C&SD National Accounts methodology:
- **Domestic Value Added** = *Gross Output minus Intermediate Consumption* generated by resident producing units in Hong Kong (GDP basis). Where cross-border financial intermediation is invoiced by overseas affiliates or global syndicate headquarters, or where equity gains accrue to non-resident shareholders, those flows belong outside Hong Kong domestic value added / GNI residency depending on institutional residency.
- Broad sector totals below capture **all** financial services (banking, insurance, asset management, brokerage, investment banking, and holding companies), providing macroeconomic context rather than an IPO-caused attribution claim.

## 2. Transaction-Time Fee & Professional-Services Anatomy (Local Audited Census)

### 2.1 Thirty-Three-Year Primary Market Underwriting & Professional Ecosystem (`N = 2,866`, 1994–2026Q3)
- **Implied Underwriting Spreads (`clean_33y_hkipo_panel_1994_2026_enriched.csv`)**: Across `1,758 / 2,866` IPOs with verified clean gross spreads (covering **`HK$4,300.65B`** of the census total `HK$5,659.4B`), implied gross underwriting commissions total **`HK$88.20B`**, a proceeds-weighted average of **`2.051%`**.
  - On the Main Board (`table_33y_gross_spread_by_epoch.csv`), median gross spreads stayed tightly anchored around **`2.5%`–`3.0%`** across epochs (`2.5%` in 2000–2007, `3.0%` in 2008–2022, `2.8%` in 2023–2026Q3), while GEM median spreads rose from `3.5%` (2000–2017) to **`7.0%`** (2018–2022) as compliance risk and small-deal placing effort escalated.
- **Big-4 Accounting Oligopoly**: Across `2,864` IPOs with non-missing reporting-accountant records in our 33-year census, **PwC (`581`), EY (`660`), Deloitte (`611`), and KPMG (`354`)**—together with legacy Big-5/6 mergers (`Arthur Andersen 61`, `Coopers & Lybrand 21`, `Price Waterhouse 16`)—audited **`2,389 / 2,866` (`83.4%`)** of all Hong Kong ordinary IPOs (`2,327` / `81.2%` strictly Big-4 names). By epoch, Big-4+AA share remained remarkably durable: **`87.5%`** (1994–2004), **`79.3%`** (2005–2017), **`84.0%`** (2018–2022), and **`86.9%`** (2023–2026Q3).
- **Property & Asset Valuers**: `1,327 / 2,866` (`46.3%`) listings carried formal external valuation reports in our census fields, anchored by Hong Kong-based surveying and corporate appraisal practices led by *Jones Lang LaSalle / Sallmanns* (`277+` combined listings across predecessor/merged entities), *DTZ / Cushman & Wakefield / C Y Leung* (`174+`), *Chesterton Petty* (`53`), *Savills* (`43`), *Vigers* (`39`), and *CBRE* (`37`).

## 3. Recurring Exchange, Custody, Data & Fiscal Revenues: The Economic Life of a Listing

An IPO is a **one-day primary event that initiates a multi-decade recurring service stream**. HKEX Group Consolidated Financial Statements demonstrate that initial and annual listing fees represent a modest minority of exchange revenues relative to continuous secondary trading, clearing, CCASS custody, market data, and margin/clearing-house investment income.

### Government Fiscal Linkage: Stock Transaction Stamp Duty (IRD / HKSAR Budget)
- **Statutory Rate Cycles**: Standard stock transfer stamp duty (`Stamp Duty Ordinance Cap. 117`) was `0.10%` per side (`0.20%` round-trip), temporarily raised to **`0.13%` per side** effective **August 1, 2021** (`2021–22` Budget) before later reduction/adjustments and targeted ETF/market-maker waivers.
- **Highly Cyclical Fiscal Collections (IRD Total Stamp Duty — Stock + Property Combined)**:
  - `FY 2020–21`: **`HK$99.7B`** (pandemic/tech equity turnover boom)
  - `FY 2021–22`: **`HK$70.0B`**
  - `FY 2022–23`: **`HK$49.1B`**
  - `FY 2024–25`: **`HK$63.9B`** (`+30.1%` YoY rebound)
  - `H1 FY 2025–26` (Apr–Sep 2025, *stock stamp duty alone*): **`~HK$44B`** (`+140%+` YoY), demonstrating that fiscal revenue tracks secondary-market turnover cycles (`Figure 4.1` / `9.1`) rather than primary IPO volume alone.

## 4. `Figure 9.1` Flow & Residency Map Skeleton (*A Dollar Raised in Hong Kong: Where Does It Go?*)

1. **Investor Cash Inflow (`N = 2,866` Ordinary IPOs: `HK$5,659.4B`, 1994–2026Q3)**:
   - **Primary vs. Secondary Tranche Split**: Across `1,862` share-decomposed deals (`HK$3,428.4B`), **`94.19%` of gross proceeds** accrued to primary new shares (`96.6%` Epoch II -> `93.4%` Epoch III -> `81.3%` Epoch IV -> **`99.8%` Epoch V**), with the balance to selling shareholders (plus the 2001–2017 Mainland `NSSF` 10% state-pension transfer rule on state H-share offerings).
2. **Transaction-Time Leakage vs. Onshore Retention**:
   - **`~4.03%` value-weighted** (`~8%–18%` on median/small deals) deducted as listing expenses -> **`50.9%` (`HK$12.27B` in `N=216`)** paid to legal, Big-4 audit, valuer, printer, registrar, and statutory fees largely anchored in Hong Kong; **`~49.1%`** to underwriting syndicates (base + discretionary), split across HK, Mainland parent securities houses, and global bookrunner hubs.
3. **Corporate Capital Deployment (Residency Separation)**:
   - Primary net proceeds enter issuer treasuries and flow predominantly to **Mainland China** or **overseas operating assets** (e.g., `CATL 3750.HK` earmarking `~90%` of `HK$40.63B` net proceeds to **Debrecen, Hungary**), alongside **`~1.1%` mean (`8.8%` deal incidence)** debt repayment (`N=221`), rather than domestic Hong Kong capex.
4. **Long-Term Host-City Value Engine**:
   - Recurring HKEX trading, clearing, custody (`CCASS`), and market data fees (`HK$19.95B` in 2025), ongoing Big-4 annual audits, follow-on corporate advisory (`Section 5.5` & `Pack 2`), and cyclical stock stamp duty.
