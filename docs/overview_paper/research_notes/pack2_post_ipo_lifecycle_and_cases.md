## 2. Five Matched Proceeds-to-Operations Case Files (`Table 10.1` Dossier)

Each case strictly answers the six diagnostic questions required by **Section 10.2** of the Master Blueprint:
1. **Pre-IPO constraint / institutional setting**
2. **Primary vs. secondary share split & listing terms (local census-verified)**
3. **Prospectus Use-of-Proceeds allocation**
4. **Post-IPO annual-report utilization & follow-on financing behavior**
5. **Real operational / capex / R&D / balance-sheet outcome**
6. **Explicit attribution limit (what the HK IPO did *and did not* cause)**

---

### Case 1 — State Bank Recapitalization: **ICBC (`1398.HK`, October 27, 2006)** *(with BOC `3988.HK` comparison)*
1. **Pre-IPO Constraint**: China's largest commercial bank exited the 1990s burdened by legacy policy-lending non-performing loans (NPLs) and thin capital adequacy—structurally incompatible with international Basel capital standards and public-market scrutiny.
2. **Primary Split & Listing Metrics (`Local Census Verified`)**: Dual A+H IPO (`Chapter 19A`); H-share offer price **`HK$3.07`**, total HK-tranche gross proceeds **`HK$124.95B`** (part of the historic `~US$21.9B` combined A+H global offering); **`2.50%` gross spread**; `15%` greenshoe; lead sponsor `CICC`; auditor `Ernst & Young`; **`+14.66%` Day-1 close**, **`+37.73%` 1-year BHAR** (`+32.84%` vs `HSI-TR`). *(Contrast BOC `3988.HK`, June 1, 2006: `HK$86.74B` pure primary `29.40B` shares @ `HK$2.95`, `+15.25%` Day-1, `-19.33%` 1-year BHAR).*
3. **Prospectus Use of Proceeds**: Explicitly designated to **strengthen the bank's core capital base and Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR)** to support asset growth and meet regulatory capital requirements following joint-stock restructuring.
4. **Post-IPO Utilization & Follow-On Lifecycle**: Proceeds immediately fortified Tier-1 equity; post-2008 rapid credit expansion later required a **major A+H rights issue in late 2010** (subscribed pro-rata by controlling shareholders Central Huijin and the Ministry of Finance) to lift CAR past **`10%–12%`** under tighter Basel III buffers.
5. **Real Balance-Sheet & Governance Outcome**: Transitioned from technical pre-reform fragility into the world's largest listed bank by market value/assets, adopting IFRS audit standards (`EY`), quarterly disclosure, and board-level risk oversight, while ranking among Hong Kong's largest net offered-tranche wealth contributors (`Section 8.5`).
6. **Attribution Boundary (`IMF 2006; BOC 2008` chain)**: The Hong Kong IPO **did not** single-handedly fix ICBC's balance sheet. Pre-IPO **Central Huijin capital injections**, **state Asset Management Company (AMC) NPL carve-outs**, **joint-stock conversion**, and pre-IPO strategic minority stakes (**Goldman Sachs, Allianz, American Express**) repaired solvency *before* listing; Hong Kong supplied the **external valuation benchmark, hard-currency capital replenishment, international governance anchor, and repeatable follow-on rights-issue platform**.

### Case 2 — Private Consumer/Tech Cash-Engine Compounder: **Tencent Holdings (`0700.HK`, June 16, 2004)**
1. **Pre-IPO Constraint**: Founded in 1998 in Shenzhen with early backing from MIH/Naspers and IDG, Tencent held massive QQ user scale in 2004 while still building monetization across mobile VAS, online gaming, and advertising—lacking a scalable domestic Chinese listing route for an offshore Cayman/VIE internet structure.
2. **Primary Split & Listing Metrics (`Local Census Verified`)**: Main Board Cayman/VIE offering; **100% pure primary** (`483,184,500` new shares including greenshoe, **`0` secondary vendor shares**); offer price **`HK$3.70`**, total gross proceeds **`HK$1.788B`** (`15%` greenshoe); lead sponsor `Goldman Sachs (Asia)`; auditor `PwC`; **`+12.16%` Day-1 close**, **`+29.63%` 1-year BHAR** (`+25.87%` vs `HSI-TR`).
3. **Prospectus Use of Proceeds (*June 7, 2004 Prospectus*)**: Estimated net proceeds of **`HK$1,258M`** pre-greenshoe (**`HK$1,454M`** post-greenshoe at mid-point pricing) deliberately carried **an open-ended strategic mandate** (*"have not currently identified any specific use of proceeds"*), reserved for organic platform expansion (messaging network, R&D, marketing) and strategic initiatives.
4. **Post-IPO Utilization & Follow-On Lifecycle**: Remarkably, across **22+ years since June 2004**, Tencent has executed **zero follow-on public equity offerings** (zero primary share dilution on HKEX). Subsequent capital needs for M&A, cloud/AI capex, and global gaming investments were financed via **internal operating cash flow** and investment-grade **Global Medium Term Note (`GMTN`) debt programs** launched from April 2014 onward.
5. **Real Operational & Wealth Outcome**: Scaled from a niche Shenzhen messaging app into a global digital ecosystem (`WeChat`, gaming, fintech, cloud), generating **`+HK$1,093.2B` in net offered-tranche price appreciation** in our census endpoint (`Section 8.5`)—single-handedly accounting for **`29.5%`** of Hong Kong's entire `+HK$3,706.0B` 33-year aggregate net equity appreciation.
6. **Attribution Boundary**: HKEX did *not* finance Tencent's multi-decade R&D out of IPO cash (`HK$1.45B` was dwarfed within years by operating cash flow); rather, Hong Kong's **1994/2000s Cayman-VIE accommodation** gave a private Mainland tech firm an early liquid equity valuation, employee-incentive currency, global institutional credibility, and debt-market access without forced onshore state ownership.

### Case 3 — Outbound Industrial Globalizer: **CATL (`3750.HK`, May 20, 2025)**
1. **Pre-IPO Constraint**: Already listed on Shenzhen ChiNext (`300750.SZ`) and holding RMB 270.16B cash (`YE 2024`, vs RMB 137.83B interest-bearing debt) on `RMB 362.01B` revenue (`RMB 96.99B` operating cash flow), CATL nonetheless needed **hard-currency offshore equity (`EUR/HKD/USD`)** insulated from Mainland FX-outflow approvals to fund multi-billion-euro European battery capacity for BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Stellantis, and VW.
2. **Primary Split & Listing Metrics (`Local Audited N=226 + 33y Census`)**: Chapter 19A A+H secondary-venue IPO; **100% pure primary** (`0` sale shares); fixed offer price **`HK$263.00`** (`-6.66%` vs A-share close); base offering **`HK$35.66B`** / post-greenshoe gross **`HK$41.01B`** (`HK$41,005.72M`, full `15%` greenshoe); **net proceeds `HK$40.63B`** (`HK$40,627.10M` per 2025 AR); base underwriting commission **`0.20%`**, total listing expenses **`HK$288.3M` (`0.81%` of base gross)**; `4` cornerstones (`1.28%` unrestricted float at listing); lead sponsor `CICC`; auditor `Grant Thornton HK`; **`+12.39%` Day-1 close** (`+16.43%` total adjusted/post-greenshoe check in `p33`), **`+60.19%` 6M return**, **`+108.04%` 1Y BHAR**.
3. **Prospectus Use of Proceeds (*May 2025 Prospectus, pp. 310–311*)**: Earmarked **`~90%` of net proceeds** directly to **Phase I and Phase II construction of its Debrecen EV-battery mega-factory in Hungary** (100 GWh planned regional supply hub), with the `~10%` balance for general corporate purposes and global working capital.
4. **Post-IPO Utilization & Follow-On Lifecycle (`2025 Annual Report, Mar 2026`)**: CATL confirmed ongoing compliance with the prospectus schedule—disbursing funds directly into Debrecen equipment procurement and plant construction—without issuing further equity; overall FY 2025 revenue grew `17%` YoY to `RMB 423.7B` and net profit reached `RMB 72.2B` (`~96.9%` capacity utilization).
5. **Real Operational Outcome**: Demonstrates Epoch V's hallmark **offshore industrial-policy use case**: using Hong Kong to raise **offshore hard currency (`HK$40.6B` / `~US$5.2B`) at institutional fee efficiency (`0.2%` base UW / `~0.8%` all-in cost)**—effectively acting as China's international capital-expenditure syndication desk.
6. **Attribution Boundary**: Because CATL already generated nearly `RMB 100B` in annual operating cash flow (`Y-1`) and traded in Shenzhen, Hong Kong did *not* create CATL's manufacturing technology; its marginal contribution was **convertible cross-border EUR/USD funding speed, global institutional investor re-pricing (creating an unusual A-to-H premium), and lower cross-border FX friction**.

### Case 4 — Chapter 18A Pre-Revenue Biotech Scaler: **Innovent Biologics (`1801.HK`, October 31, 2018)**
1. **Pre-IPO Constraint**: Capital-intensive biologics developer advancing PD-1 monoclonal antibody (*sintilimab / Tyvyt*) and multi-asset clinical trials with virtually **zero commercial revenue (`HK$4.04M` Y-1 in census)**, structurally barred from profitable Main Board `Rule 8.05` and A-share profitability gates prior to 2018.
2. **Primary Split & Listing Metrics (`Local Census Verified`)**: Early **Chapter 18A** pre-revenue biotech IPO; offer price **`HK$13.98`**, gross proceeds **`HK$3.80B`** (`~US$421M` pre-shoe); `3.0%` gross spread; `4.05%` cornerstone share; lead sponsor `Morgan Stanley Asia`; auditor `Deloitte`; **`+18.60%` Day-1 close**, **`+36.44%` 1-year BHAR** (`+32.50%` vs `HSI-TR`).
3. **Prospectus Use of Proceeds**: Allocated predominantly to advancing Phase II/III clinical trials and regulatory NDA filings for *sintilimab* and core pipeline antibodies, expanding Suzhou manufacturing/GMP quality capacity, and funding discovery R&D.
4. **Post-IPO Utilization & Follow-On Lifecycle**: Crucially, the IPO served as **Stage 1 of a multi-round public equity funding ladder**: Innovent repeatedly returned to HKEX's fast general-mandate **top-up placement** market (`2019–2025`, including a **`HK$4.31B` gross / `HK$4.27B` net placing in June 2025**), supplementing equity with global pharma out-licensing deals (`Takeda`, `Pfizer` milestones).
5. **Real Operational Outcome**: Commercialized multiple NRDL-reimbursed therapies and metabolic/GLP-1 assets (*mazdutide*), crossing **`RMB 10 billion` total revenue and achieving its first full-year net profit in FY 2025**—evolving from a cash-burning Chapter 18A R&D entity into a profitable commercial biopharma company.
6. **Attribution Boundary**: Chapter 18A listing rules enabled *early risk-sharing before profit*, while HKEX's permissive **post-IPO secondary placing mechanism (`Section 5.5`)** financed multi-year clinical attrition; however, *Innovent represents a right-tail survivor* among 18A biotechs whose outcome depended equally on clinical trial execution, NRDL reimbursement access, and partner licensing validation.

### Case 5 — Post-IPO Reversal & Capital-Allocation Counter-Example: **Razer Inc. (`1337.HK`, Nov 13, 2017)** *(with Ascletis `1672.HK` & Shinsun `2599.HK` contrast notes)*
1. **Pre-IPO Constraint**: Loss-making Singapore/US gaming hardware & peripherals brand backed by high-profile tech/PE sponsors, seeking Hong Kong consumer-tech growth multiples ahead of Chapter 8A/18C to subsidize diversification into smartphones and fintech/virtual credits.
2. **Primary Split & Listing Metrics (`Local Census Verified`)**: Main Board conventional IPO; offer price **`HK$3.88`**, gross proceeds **`HK$4.746B`** (`~US$596M` net); `1.75%` gross spread; heavy **`25.15%` cornerstone allocation**; lead sponsor `Credit Suisse (HK)`; auditor `KPMG`; **`+18.04%` Day-1 close**, followed by **`-60.87%` 1-year BHAR** (`-63.66%` vs `HSI-TR`).
3. **Prospectus Use of Proceeds**: Earmarked to fund **new hardware verticals (mobile gaming devices)**, **R&D and strategic acquisitions**, brand marketing, and expansion of its **integrated hardware-software-services ecosystem**.
4. **Post-IPO Utilization & Follow-On Lifecycle**: Instead of raising follow-on growth equity at higher multiples, depressed trading forced value-destructive retreat from high-burn verticals (mobile phone division shuttered/scaled back) and repeated share buybacks, culminating in a founder-plus-CVC **scheme-of-arrangement privatization and delisting on May 13, 2022 at `HK$2.82/share`**—**`-27.3%` below the `HK$3.88` IPO price** and triggering vocal minority-shareholder criticism.
5. **Real Operational Outcome**: Capital mobilization successfully transferred **`HK$4.75B`** into corporate experiments, but secondary-market price discovery penalized non-core diversification and low profitability, resulting in permanent public-market exit at a discount to IPO buyers.
6. **Comparative Cross-Sector Counter-Examples in Our Census**:
   - *Chapter 18A Failure Mode (`Ascletis Pharma 1672.HK`, Aug 1, 2018)*: The **very first Chapter 18A biotech IPO** raised `HK$3.14B` (`HK$14.00`, `0%` Day-1, **`-63.48%` 1Y BHAR**), pivoted repeatedly across stalled antiviral/NASH pipelines into GLP-1 obesity trials (`ASC30`), yet recorded only **`RMB 1.28M` (2024) / `RMB 2.03M` product revenue (2025)** alongside **`RMB 301M–360M` annual net losses** seven years post-IPO.
   - *2020 Property Leverage Collapse (`Shinsun Holdings 2599.HK`, Nov 18, 2020)*: Raised `HK$3.35B` (`0.0%` Day-1, **`-66.94%` 1Y BHAR**) at peak Mainland developer debt (`RMB 243.9B` assets) right before "Three Red Lines" tightening triggered sector-wide default and listing distress.
   - **Combined Lesson**: Hong Kong's IPO gate prices *prospectus promise* and supplies liquidity, but neither screens out competitive failure (`Razer`), guarantees R&D translation (`Ascletis`), nor shields issuers from macro-regulatory shocks (`Shinsun`)—explaining why **`77.47%` of all IPOs trade below `P_0` at endpoint** (`Section 8.5`) while right-tail winners (`Tencent, ICBC, CATL, Innovent`) dominate aggregate wealth.
