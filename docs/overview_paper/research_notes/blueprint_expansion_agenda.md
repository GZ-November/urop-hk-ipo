# Expanding the HK IPO Blueprint: History, Economic Functions, and Public Value

Prepared 2026-11-17. This is a research and editorial agenda, not a completed causal impact study or a revision of the underlying IPO data. Existing manuscripts remain unchanged. See also `hk_domestic_value_sources.md` for the companion domestic-history source note.

## Editorial decision

The user's objective extends beyond a review of IPO mechanics to a history of what the market has contributed to Hong Kong, Mainland China, global investors, and financial institutions. Preserve the continuous historical narrative and existing empirical modules, but add explicit economic-function and distributional chapters. Build the comprehensive research master before condensing a 40–45-page review edition.

## Evidence and measurement boundaries

- IPO gross proceeds, issuer net proceeds, locally produced service value added, tax receipts and investor capital gains are different quantities; never sum them into a single GDP contribution.
- Financial-sector GDP and employment describe the surrounding economy, not the portion caused by IPOs. Supplier fees cannot all be assigned to Hong Kong, and fees are not themselves value added.
- Track primary capital, secondary shareholder sales, post-IPO issuance and buybacks separately. Preserve geographical destination and recipient identity where available.
- Announced use of proceeds establishes intent. Subsequent disclosures establish implementation. Neither alone establishes additional investment caused by listing.
- Separate offering allocations, market turnover, custody positions, fund AUM and ultimate beneficial ownership. Sponsor nationality does not identify investors' capital origin; CCASS is not a full beneficial-owner register.
- Historical relevance can extend beyond the statistical census. Funds, REITs, introductions, failed listings and pre-1994 episodes need a separate ledger rather than changes to the ordinary-equity IPO denominator.
- Successful IPOs are selected from applications and potential issuers. Include failures and alternative routes when discussing access and competitiveness.
- Record historical rules as they applied at the event date; present-day rules cannot be projected backwards.
- Exchange speeches and issuer narratives are valuable primary accounts of intention and institutional memory, but not independent proof of causal success.

## Primary-source anchors checked in this session

1. [HKEX 2000 listing document, History and Development](https://www.hkexgroup.com/-/media/HKEX-Group-Site/ssd/Investor-Relations/circulars/documents/e_388pro20000622chap11.pdf). Records the 1921 second exchange and 1947 merger. Supports filling the early-history gap; do not describe the entire 1891–1968 period as a single unchanged exchange monopoly.
2. [HKMA, Linked Exchange Rate System](https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/key-functions/money/linked-exchange-rate-system/). Dates the system to 17 October 1983 and describes its currency-board arrangements. Pair monetary stability with research on interest-rate transmission; do not claim equity risk disappears.
3. [Basic Law, Chapter V](https://www.basiclaw.gov.hk/en/basiclaw/chapter5.html/index.html). Articles 109–112 address the financial centre, monetary arrangements, convertibility and capital flows. Institutional foundation, not a causal estimate of market success.
4. [C&SD, Financial Services in Hong Kong, December 2025 digest](https://www.censtatd.gov.hk/en/data/stat_report/product/B1010002/att/B10100022025MM12B0100.pdf). Reports 2024 financial-services value added of HK$816.2 billion, 26.2% of GDP, and 265,000 jobs, 7.2% of employment. These are sector-wide context, not IPO attribution.
5. [HKEX 2024 Annual Report](https://www.hkexgroup.com/-/media/HKEX-Group-Site/ssd/Investor-Relations/Regulatory-Reports/documents/2025/250317ar_e_WCAG.pdf), Business Review p.47. Separately reports IPO and post-IPO fundraising: Main Board HK$87.8bn and HK$102.5bn respectively; GEM HK$0.2bn and HK$1.7bn. Figures surfaced in indexed report text; full report fetch exceeded the browser size limit. A future data build should download and reconcile revisions against preliminary annual market statistics, not mix vintages.
6. [HKEX, H-shares: kick-starting 30 years of connectivity, Laura Cha, 2023](https://www.hkexgroup.com/Media-Centre/Insight/Insight/2023/Laura-Cha/H-shares-kick-starting-30-years-of-connectivity?sc_lang=en). First-person account of institutional negotiation, Mandatory Provisions, opposition and governance goals. Attribute claims about transformative success to the author; verify actual governance outcomes separately.
7. [IMF, Progress in China's Banking Sector Reform: Has Bank Behavior Changed?, 2006](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/001/2006/071/article-A001-en.xml). Search-index extract documents recapitalization, joint-stock transformation, governance and listing as distinct stages. Full-page browser access returned 403; treat as a research lead for full-text retrieval, not completed review.
8. [Bank of China, account of its 2006 listing](https://www.boc.cn/en/aboutboc/ab7/200809/t20080926_1601843.html). Primary corporate retrospective linking listing to shareholding reform and ownership diversification; pair with prospectus accounts and independent analysis.
9. [CATL final Hong Kong prospectus, 12 May 2025](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2025/0512/2025051200005.pdf). Summary pp.16–17 and Future Plans and Use of Proceeds pp.310–311 provide an explicit bridge from listing to international operations and Hungary investment. Match planned spending to later reports before claiming realized outcomes.
10. [HKEX survey archive](https://www.hkex.com.hk/News/Research-Reports/HKEX-Surveys/HKEX-Surveys?sc_lang=en) and [2019 Cash Market Transaction Survey](https://www.hkex.com.hk/-/media/HKEX-Market/News/Research-Reports/HKEX-Surveys/Cash-Market-Transaction-Survey-2019/CMTS2019_e.pdf). Historical trading composition by investor type; these measure trading activity, not IPO subscriptions or ultimate ownership.
11. [SFC, Asset and Wealth Management Activities Survey 2024](https://www.sfc.hk/-/media/EN/files/COM/Reports-and-surveys/EN_AWMAS-2024.pdf?hash=4DB3FE796C762C7DD3117EDBEAA3A1D9&rev=a2f62073afd345078c352e65c7492b72). Reports 63% non-Hong Kong investor assets and 54% from outside both Mainland China and Hong Kong, subject to the stated REIT/trust exclusions. Illustrates international asset-management context; it is not an IPO capital-origin measure.
12. [HKEX, Hong Kong's role as a fundraising hub for international companies, 2023](https://www.hkexgroup.com/Media-Centre/Insight/Insight/2023/HKEX-Insight/Hong-Kongs-role-as-a-fundraising-hub-for-international-companies?sc_lang=en). Research framing on international venue choices and two-way connectivity. Test its hypotheses using individual listings and continuing liquidity rather than assuming internationalization follows from admission.
13. [SFC, Hontex ordered to make HK$1.03bn buy-back offer, 2012](https://apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/api/news/list-content?lang=EN&refNo=12PR63). Concrete investor-redress case for an enforcement chapter; distinguish the court-ordered remedy from allegations about the underlying financial statements.
14. [HKEX circular, Ant listing suspension, 4 November 2020](https://www.hkex.com.hk/-/media/HKEX-Market/Services/Circulars-and-Notices/Participant-and-Members-Circulars/SEHK/2020/MKS_EQD_31_20_e.pdf). An important historical event outside the completed IPO census. Use official records to reconstruct suspension, funding/refund mechanics and the division of regulatory authority.

## Suggested research sequence

1. Add historical gaps and separate the ordinary-equity census from the broader historical event ledger.
2. Build three evidence tables: Hong Kong retained services and income; Mainland issuer financing and real investment; global investor access and risk-bearing.
3. Construct matched issuer case files: pre-listing constraints, offering recipient, use-of-proceeds plan, subsequent implementation, repeat financing, investor outcomes and unresolved counterfactual.
4. Add demand-side and enforcement histories, including failed offerings and alternative venues.
5. Synthesize the value and trade-offs only after distinguishing demonstrated outcomes, institutional mechanisms, descriptive associations and unanswered causal questions.

## Possible expanded architecture

1. Introduction: what the market made possible, and for whom.
2. Historical Part: complete chronological development, keeping monetary, political, industrial and exchange institutions connected.
3. Institutional Part: issuer access, ownership, intermediaries, pricing, allocation, settlement and aftermarket outcomes.
4. Economic Part: Hong Kong's service economy and households; Mainland corporate transformation and repeat financing; global investors and international issuers.
5. Synthesis: enforcement, market competition, distribution of gains, institutional trade-offs and research questions.

Candidate title: *Hong Kong's IPO Market: A Financial History of the City, China, and Global Capital, 1891–2026*.
