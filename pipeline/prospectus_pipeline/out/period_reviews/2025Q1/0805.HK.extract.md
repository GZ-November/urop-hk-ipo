- **Period Mapping (Fixed financial-period standard)**:
  - **year-1**: 6 months ended 30 June 2024 (6M2024), `col_AT = 30/06/24`.

  - **year-3**: Full financial year ended 31 December 2022 (FY2022).

Per teacher's manual §2.3 and rule 3 ("Never solve for a number to make assets = equity + liabilities hold; if a number cannot be found, use NaN"), col_W, col_X, col_Y and col_AC, col_AD, col_AE are set to NaN. Total equity (col_Z, col_AA, col_AB) is explicitly reported and extracted from the "TOTAL EQUITY" line.
  - **Unannualised items**: AU, AV, AW, AX, AY, BE, CF, CG take 6M2024 or 30 June 2024 values without annualisation.
