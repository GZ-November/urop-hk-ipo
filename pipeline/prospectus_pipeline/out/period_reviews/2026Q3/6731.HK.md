### col_BE judgement (redemption liabilities)

I agree with leaving out redemption liabilities. They are 1,738,922 thousand at 30/06/2026 and come from preferred or redemption rights held by pre-IPO investors. They are recorded at carrying amount, with changes going through profit or loss as "Changes in the carrying amount of the redemption liabilities". They are not interest-bearing borrowings and are expected to be reclassified to equity on listing.

Including them would make col_BE (1,790,271 thousand, the full table Total) about 97% pre-IPO investor instruments. That would distort any leverage measure. Amounts due to a related party were nil at 30/06/2026, so they make no difference.

If the research lead wants the literal INDEBTEDNESS Total instead, the value would be 1,790,271,000 (p237, "Total ... 1,790,271"). I do not recommend this.
