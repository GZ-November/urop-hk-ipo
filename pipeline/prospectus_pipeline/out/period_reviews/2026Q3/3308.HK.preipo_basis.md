## Decision: is col_BA = NaN acceptable, or must it be 1?
NaN is acceptable; 1 is not required. This withdraws the 'should be 1' suggestion in my first report, which was a judgement call I flagged for adjudication.
- The col_BA hint is: 1 if a Pre-IPO VC/PE investor was introduced; 'as long as a professional investment institution (VC/PE/industry fund) bought in before listing' it is 1. The trigger is the investor being a VC/PE/industry-fund type, not merely an institution that invested.
- The prospectus names Google Capital (Hong Kong) Limited and the date, but never describes what kind of investor it is. The sibling hints require the classification basis to appear in the field's own quote (col_vc_backed) and forbid inferring from reputation (col_top_tier_vc). Calling Google Capital a VC/PE/CVC would rely on outside knowledge.
- The investment was in the operating subsidiary Suzhou InnoLight before its 2017 acquisition, not a subscription for the issuer's own shares, which weakens it as an 'issuer's own pre-IPO' investment.
- The field hint also says missing evidence is not 0, and the validator rule requires NaN when neither 0 nor 1 can be shown. 0 is impossible (an investor is named); 1 is unproven; so NaN is the only supportable value. If a later source classifies Google Capital, col_BA and the type flags should be revisited together.
