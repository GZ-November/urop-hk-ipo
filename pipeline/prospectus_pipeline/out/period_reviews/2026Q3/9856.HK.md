### col_BE: whether to exclude redemption liabilities

I agree with excluding them. They are the issuer's obligations to redeem shares held by pre-IPO investors under redemption rights. The prospectus discusses them under redemption arrangements, not under “Interest-bearing Bank and Other Borrowings”, and they are not interest-bearing debt. The field is defined as interest-bearing debt. Excluding them also matches the basis of the previously reviewed Dec-2025 col_BE: 87,221 = 626,173 − 538,952, which is the lease liabilities only.

If the research lead decides that the literal INDEBTEDNESS Total is required, the alternative is col_BE = 2,409,394,000 (p228, quote “Total 1,664,892 3,326,427 626,173 2,409,394 2,446,586”). The rest of the payload is unaffected either way.
