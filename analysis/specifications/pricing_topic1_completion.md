# Topic 1 completion specification

Declared 4 October 2026, after Steps 1-4 and the lower-bound audit. These are
post-exploration extensions, not preregistered confirmatory tests. The objective
is to complete the current 2026 pricing-stage study, with honest unresolved
identification limits. Topics 2 and 3 are excluded from its final report.

Input: the frozen 113-issuer pricing panel and the 31-issuer disclosure audit.
Keep all 43 two-sided-range offers for every primary price-stage regression.
Apply the audit as a research overlay: 30 maximum-only and one single price.
Do not amend extraction JSON or workbooks. Equal endpoints in the other 39
records remain a recorded classification, not a fresh full-source certification.

1. Main equation: log offer-to-close on fractional midpoint revision and Q2/Q3.
Opening, intraday and subscription-close HSI-adjusted close are companion
outcomes on identical firms and controls. Reproduce HC3 and exact restricted
Rademacher cluster-t (512 signs). Keep the original seven-outcome Holm family
for primary inference; do not narrow it to obtain smaller adjusted p-values.
2. Month dependence: delete each of nine listing months, report coefficients,
regressor information shares and full-design CV3 jackknife covariance. Check
CV3 against the adjusted block-residual sandwich. t reference has G-1=8 df.
Report interval and p-value limits; no stars and no simulated resampling.
3. Revision geometry: add range width to the quarter model. Separately replace
revision by centered range position, conditional on range width and quarters.
Because revision=width*(position-0.5), these estimate different functionals.
4. Exploratory asymmetry: positive and negative parts of revision with quarters.
Test both slopes and their difference for four stage outcomes. Apply Holm to
all 12 exact wild-cluster tests in this new family. Low sample support limits
interpretation. Never select a specification because it is significant.
5. Public-market information: regress revision on prospectus-close to
subscription-close HSI movement and quarters. This uses a named calendar
window, not verified pre-agreement information; do not label it a causal
price-incorporation test. Report the closing-stage equation with this market
measure as a companion check.
6. Date sensitivity without dropping companies: obtain all candidate HSI closes
between (a) subscription close and final-price publication and (b) prospectus
publication and final-price publication, including the latest trading close
on or before the start date. Calculate issuer outcome intervals and sharp
conditional linear-slope bounds by FWL weights. Independently verify the
bounds with linear programming. These are sensitivity sets, not certified
actual pricing intervals or confidence intervals. All 43 issuers remain.
7. Maximum-only offers: describe ceiling discounts and returns. Report each
below-ceiling case, the 26-at-ceiling concentration if reproduced, listing
route composition and within-A+H descriptions. Do not impute a midpoint or fit
a supposedly informative model driven by only four below-ceiling observations.

Report supported findings, plausible competing explanations, unresolved dates,
selection, range constraints, benchmark limitations and failure to establish
causal information incorporation. The current data analysis can be completed
without claiming that the economic cause of every first-day jump is identified.
