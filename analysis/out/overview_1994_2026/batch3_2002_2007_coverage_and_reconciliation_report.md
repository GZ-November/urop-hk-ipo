2. **Fixed Financial Period Standard (§2.3)**: Flow variables (`col_AH`, `col_AK`, `col_AN`) are annualized (`* 12.0 / months`) when reported in stub audit periods (<12 months), with exact factors and month counts recorded in verbatim quotes.
