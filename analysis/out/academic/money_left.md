# Money left on the table, 2026 (N = 106)

MLOT = (day-1 close - offer price) x base offer shares. Fees = the disclosed underwriting commission on HK and international proceeds
(discretionary incentive fees are not in the data and would add up to 1%).

| Sample | N | Net MLOT (HK$bn) | Underwriting fees (HK$bn) | MLOT / fees | Gains on deals with IR > 0 (HK$bn) | Losses on deals with IR < 0 (HK$bn) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| All 2026 | 106 | 83.0 | 4.8 | 17.3x | 93.7 | -10.7 |
| April-June listings | 45 | 57.2 | 1.6 | 34.8x | 61.3 | -4.1 |
| Other listings | 61 | 25.8 | 3.2 | 8.2x | 32.4 | -6.6 |

- In aggregate the money left is 17.3 times the fees paid; on 65% of deals the day-1 gain exceeds the underwriting fee.
  Median deal-level ratio is 8.8x. Net figures hide 10.7bn of losses on deals that closed below the offer price.

## Who holds the gain

|  | All 2026 | April-June listings | Other listings |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cornerstone investors: share of shares | 39.0% | 39.5% | 38.5% |
| Other placees: share of shares | 50.1% | 49.7% | 50.5% |
| Retail (public offer): share of shares | 10.9% | 10.8% | 11.0% |
| Cornerstone investors: share of net MLOT | 40.6% | 40.1% | 41.6% |
| Other placees: share of net MLOT | 46.9% | 47.7% | 45.3% |
| Retail (public offer): share of net MLOT | 12.5% | 12.1% | 13.1% |

- Cornerstone investors hold 39.0% of the base offer and capture 40.6% of net money left; retail holds 10.9% of the shares and captures 12.5%.
  Shares held and gains match closely because IR is a per-share return: the allocation, not the pricing, decides who benefits, and the public tranche is small.
- Fee rate (fees / proceeds) and IR: Spearman = 0.21 (p = 0.03). Small deals carry both higher fee rates and higher IR, so the raw correlation mostly
  reflects size: with ln size, the window and A+H held fixed, the commission-rate coefficient on log(1 + IR) is -0.104 pp (HC3 p = 0.45).

## What a retail application earns

Expected gain per HK$10,000 = allocation ratio (final public shares / valid applied shares, capped at 1) x IR x 10,000, assuming proportional allocation
(actual allocation is by lottery, so this is an expectation, not a typical outcome).

|  | N | Median allocation ratio | Expected gain per HK$10,000 applied, median | Expected gain per HK$10,000 applied, mean | Deals with an expected loss | Retail gain per applicant, median (HK$) | Retail gain per applicant, mean (HK$) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Other listings | 61 | 0.23% | HK$0.7 | HK$4.6 | 31% | 76 | 290 |
| April-June listings | 45 | 0.06% | HK$2.6 | HK$-7.1 | 22% | 401 | 533 |

- The typical application earns a few Hong Kong dollars per HK$10,000; the mean is driven by rare large allocations and losses on broken issues.
  Gain per applicant averages the retail gain over everyone who applied, winners and non-winners.
